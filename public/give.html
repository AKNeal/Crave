Giving back

Hometown Crave

Crave keeps growing, and we want that growth to do some good right here at home. This school year we're putting real energy behind North Pole kids: their schools, sports, clubs and activities.

If North Pole keeps showing up for Crave, Crave is going to keep showing up for North Pole.

Partner with usSee how it works

Ways we can help

Pick one, or dream up your own

Percentage-of-sales days

Pick a day, spread the word, and a share of what North Pole buys at Crave that day goes to your school or team.

Team fundraisers

Cinnamon roll pans, cookie boxes and treat sales your players can take orders for, baked by us and picked up here.

PTA support

Teacher-appreciation trays, coffee for conference nights, treats for the bake-sale table, and help where your PTA needs it.

Special events

Game days, concerts, science fairs, end-of-season parties. Tell us what's coming and we'll find a way to be part of it.

Your idea here

Got something we haven't thought of? We'd love to hear it. The best ideas come from the people closest to the kids.

How it works

  1. 1

    Come talk to us

    Send the form below, call, or stop by the counter. Tell us who you are and what you have in mind.

  2. 2

    We plan it together

    We'll work out the date, what we bake or serve, and how your group will spread the word.

  3. 3

    North Pole shows up

    Your families come in, eat well, and the good stays right here at home.
