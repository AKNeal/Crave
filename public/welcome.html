  1. A07Start 7:50a1× Crave BurritoStart cooking

    Orders show up on "Today"

    When someone orders Crave Curbside, it appears under Up next. Tap the big brown button as you go:

    Start cooking → Ready for pickup → Picked up

    The time on each order tells you when to start so it's hot right at pickup. Tap This week to see upcoming days.

  2. Needs youSarah · new requestNPHS Volleyball · Hometown Crave

    Catering, cakes & Hometown Crave

    New requests show on the right under Needs you. Tap one to read it, then send an offer (price and date), reply, or decline.

    Their offer and your counter-offer go back and forth until you both agree, and then it's on your calendar.

  3. Settings☑ Take online ordersOrders per pickup time: 3Closed days

    Busy day? Closed day? Use Settings

    Next to Today, tap Settings to:

    • Pause online orders (untick Take online orders)
    • Add closed days, like holidays
    • Change how many orders fit in each pickup time
    • Post a note, like "Cinnamon rolls are sold out"
  4. Crave

    Put it on your phone

    iPhone: open the staff page in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with the arrow), then Add to Home Screen.

    Android: open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.

    Now Crave sits on your phone like an app.
