How booking works

Tell us about it

Date, headcount, how you want it served. Name a budget if you have one.

Get an offer

We send a price, menu and time. Accept it, counter it, or ask a question. It goes back and forth until it's right.

It's on the calendar

Accepting puts your event on our calendar. You get a confirmation and a private link to follow it.

Request catering

Good to know

How much notice do you need?

At least 3 days for most orders. Short notice? Send the request anyway and we'll tell you what we can do.

Do you deliver?

Pick "Delivery" on the request and add the address. Any delivery or set-up cost is listed on your offer before you accept.

Is there a deposit?

Larger orders are held with a deposit that comes off the final bill. The amount and terms are on your offer.

Can you handle allergies?

Tell us in the request and we'll label and pack those portions separately.

When do I confirm the headcount?

Final count is due 48 hours before your event.
