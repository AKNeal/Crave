Behind the counter

A small family kitchen with a big oven.

Crave is a family-run team. Between the counter, catering, special orders and contract work (and our kids), every day is full, and we open as many days as we can.

That means some of the best things happen on short notice: special open days, pop-up bakes, breakfast and lunch specials, and treats that are gone by noon. Watch this page and @crave.907 for what's coming out of the oven next.
